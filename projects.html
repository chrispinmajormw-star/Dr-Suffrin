Projects

What I have built, implemented or led. Each project is written as a short case: the problem, the intervention, how it was studied, and what I am learning.

How I work

From problem to evidence to implementation to evaluation. Every project below follows some version of this cycle.

  1. Identify the health-system problemStart from what is failing patients
  2. Understand the contextData, people, infrastructure
  3. Design an interventionGrounded in evidence
  4. Implement with frontline teamsBuilt with the people who run it
  5. Measure what changesRoutine and study data
  6. Study why it worked, or didn’tMixed methods
  7. AdaptAnd start again

AI-supported chest X-ray screening

Role Your roleSetting Rural MalawiIn progress

The problem

Symptom screening alone misses many people with TB, and radiologists are scarce in rural districts.

The intervention

Implemented AI-supported chest X-ray screening within a rural health system, linked to referral and GeneXpert confirmation.

Implementation focus

Screening workflow, referral pathway, diagnostic capacity, power and connectivity, and linkage to confirmatory testing.

What I’m learning

The accuracy of the algorithm matters less than the pathway it sits in.

Adolescent HIV services & social support

Role Your roleSetting Rural Malawi

The problem

Adolescents living with HIV face lower viral suppression and higher loss to follow-up than adults.

The intervention

Supported expansion of adolescent HIV services, including Teen Clubs and social-support interventions.

Research

Teen club model and HIV outcomes among adolescents, published in BMJ Open.

What I’m learning

Clinical outcomes for adolescents are tied to school, money, family and peer support.

Digital health systems: EMR, DHIS2 and data visualization

Role Your roleSetting District health system, Malawi

The problem

Data was collected for reporting upward more than for decisions at the facility.

The work

Worked across EMR, DHIS2 and data visualization systems to make program data usable by frontline teams.

What I’m learning

A dashboard only matters if someone uses it to change what happens on Monday morning.

Outputs

e.g. a public-safe TB cascade dashboard (screened → presumptive → tested → diagnosed → treated).
