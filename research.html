Community accountability and TB case finding

Can community scorecards improve TB case notification in rural health systems?

Background

TB case detection in the district remained below the level needed to interrupt transmission. Many people with TB symptoms never reach a facility that can test them, and the gap is shaped as much by community trust and service responsiveness as by diagnostics.

Research question

Does giving communities a structured way to assess and discuss TB services, through community scorecards and community sputum collection points, increase the number of people diagnosed and notified?

Design

A mixed-methods evaluation combining routine notification data over time with qualitative work on how communities and health workers experience the scorecard process.

AI-enabled TB screening

How can AI-supported chest X-ray screening be integrated into decentralized TB case finding?

Background

AI software can now flag chest X-rays suggestive of TB in seconds. But an abnormal image is only the start: the patient still has to give sputum, the sample has to reach a GeneXpert machine, and the result has to come back to someone who can start treatment.

What I look at

  • The screening workflow and who reads, confirms and acts on an AI result
  • The referral pathway from abnormal image to confirmatory testing
  • Diagnostic capacity downstream of the screening point
  • Infrastructure: power, connectivity and equipment maintenance
  • Linkage to bacteriological confirmation and treatment
  • Operational lessons and implementation barriers

Adolescent HIV care

How can health and social interventions improve outcomes for adolescents living with HIV?

Background

Adolescents living with HIV have some of the poorest outcomes in the HIV response. Viral suppression depends on adherence, and adherence depends on things clinics rarely control: school, money, family and belonging.

What I connect

  • Teen Clubs and peer support
  • Viral suppression and adherence
  • Social support, education and economic vulnerability
  • Community structures around the adolescent

Outputs

Teen club model and adolescent HIV outcomes, BMJ Open. See the publication summary.

From the field to the paper

One real-world problem, followed from the clinic to the research question it produced. It shows how I work: start with what is going wrong for patients, respond as a practitioner, then study the response as a researcher.

  1. The field problem

    TB patients were not being identified early enough. People with symptoms stayed in the community for weeks before reaching a facility that could test them.

  2. The program response

    Community sputum collection points and active case finding brought the first step of diagnosis closer to where people live.

  3. The research question

    Does community accountability, through community scorecards, improve TB case notification?

  4. The study

    A mixed-methods evaluation with an interrupted time-series analysis of notification data.
