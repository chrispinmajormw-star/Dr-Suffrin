From the field to the paper

How one problem in the real world became a research question. This is the path I try to follow with every piece of work: practitioner, then researcher, then implementation scientist.

  2. The program response

    Community-based sputum collection and active case finding.

  3. The research question

    Does community accountability improve case notification?

  4. The study

    A mixed-methods and interrupted time-series evaluation.

  5. The result

  6. The implication

    What health systems should do differently.
